Peanut & tree nut allergy

I have a peanut and tree nut allergy. Even a small amount could cause a severe allergic reaction requiring medical attention.

NO PEANUTS
NO TREE NUTS

I cannot eat peanuts or tree nuts

This includes their oils, extracts and flours. They may be in pesto, sauces, spreads and baked goods.

Tree nuts I need to avoid include

  • Almond
  • Hazelnut
  • Macadamia nut
  • Brazil nut
  • Pistachio
  • Cashew
  • Pecan
  • Walnut

Prevent cross-contact

Please use clean gloves, utensils, surfaces, cookware and frying oil when preparing my meal. Thank you!

Does this food contain peanuts or tree nuts?

Please check the ingredients and how it is prepared before serving.

Clarification: these are OK for me

  • Sesame
  • Pine nuts
  • Coconut

I can eat these ingredients only if they have not come into contact with peanuts or tree nuts. For example, pesto made with pine nuts is not automatically safe: please check every ingredient and how it was prepared.
